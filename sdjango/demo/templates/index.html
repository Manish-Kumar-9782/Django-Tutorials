5 effective tips to avoid acidity in between meals

When the sphincter muscle at the bottom of the esophagus relaxes inappropriately, stomach acid can reflux back up into the esophagus. Heartburn and other symptoms may result from this.Acidity between meals is often caused by excess stomach acid, which can irritate the lining of the stomach or esophagus. This occurs when the stomach continues to produce acid even without food to digest.According to Dr. Pradeepta Sethy, Director, Gastroenterology, Medica Super Specialty Hospital, “Common causes include irregular eating habits, stress, smoking, and excessive intake of caffeine, spicy or fatty foods. Conditions like gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) and peptic ulcers can also contribute. Skipping meals can trigger acidity, as the stomach empties and becomes more sensitive to its own acid. Overuse of painkillers like NSAIDs may worsen the condition by irritating the stomach lining.”Dr. Udipta Ray, Director Robotic, GI and General Surgery, Anandapur, Fortis Kolkata shares tips to relieve acidity in between meals:
  1. Avoid fasting for more than two hours at a time. It's best to have small meals or snacks every two hours, preferably bland foods.
  2. Take your time while eating—don’t rush. Chew your food thoroughly and savor the taste.
  3. Stay away from acidic and aerated foods; instead, rely more on curds, milkshakes, or yogurt, as they aid digestion.
  4. Reduce the use of excess oil in your cooking and aim to prepare your curries with less.
  5. Focus on enjoying yourself and keeping stress at bay, as worry and tension can increase acidity and negatively impact your.
